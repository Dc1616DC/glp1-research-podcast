**Alex:** This is interesting because usually when we see something that affects both vascular calcification and bones, there's a trade-off, right? Like vitamin K antagonists that can increase vascular calcification while affecting bone metabolism.

**Dan:** Exactly. What makes this protein unique is that it's beneficial for both systems. The therapeutic implications are really exciting - they showed that gene therapy to increase FAM19A5 could treat both conditions simultaneously in mice.

**Dan:** Not yet. This is still preclinical research, but it's pointing toward adipose tissue as a much more sophisticated endocrine organ than we previously understood.
