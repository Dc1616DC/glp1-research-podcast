Dan: What they found was that patients who started with incremental PD had about a 60% lower risk of losing their remaining kidney function compared to those who went straight to full-dose dialysis. And importantly, this didn't increase their risk of needing to switch to hemodialysis or dying.

Alex: The other study looked specifically at cognitive effects in older adults with diabetes. What did they find?

Alex: How much improvement are we talking about?

Dan: Yes, and there are plausible biological mechanisms. GLP-1 receptors exist in the brain, and these drugs may have direct neuroprotective effects beyond just improving glucose control.
